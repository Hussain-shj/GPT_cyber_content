# LinkedIn AI Studio — people/attire visual policy

When AI-generated LinkedIn visuals include people, use only professional formal business attire or authentic Emirati national dress. Emirati men should wear a white kandura with white ghutra and black agal. Emirati women should wear a modest black abaya and shayla/headscarf. Avoid casual clothing, generic Gulf costumes, or uncovered hair for Emirati women. This policy is intended to be embedded in both LinkedIn visual-generation prompts.
